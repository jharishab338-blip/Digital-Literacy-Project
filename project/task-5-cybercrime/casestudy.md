Case Study: Phishing Attack on a College Student (Unique Scenario)

Phishing is a cybercrime where attackers impersonate trusted sources to steal sensitive information such as login credentials, OTPs, or financial details.

In this case, a first-year engineering student received a message on WhatsApp claiming to be from a well-known internship portal. The message stated that the student had been shortlisted for a paid internship and needed to “verify their account” within 30 minutes. A link was provided that opened a professional-looking website with the company logo and login page.

Step-by-step, the attack unfolded as follows: first, the attacker created a fake website identical to the real internship platform. Next, they sent bulk messages to students collected from online groups. When the student entered their email and password, the data was captured instantly. The site then asked for an OTP “for verification,” which was actually used to access the student’s email account. Within a short time, the attacker reset passwords of linked accounts, including social media and a UPI app.

College students are frequent targets because they actively seek internships and opportunities, making such messages believable. The consequences included loss of account access, misuse of personal data, and unauthorized transactions.

This case highlights the importance of verifying job offers, avoiding suspicious links, and never sharing OTPs or login details online.
