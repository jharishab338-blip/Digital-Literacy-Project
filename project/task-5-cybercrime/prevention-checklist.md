 Stay Safe Online – Checklist for College Students (India)

Do’s
- Use strong, unique passwords for all accounts and enable two-factor authentication (2FA).
- Always verify website URLs before entering login details (check for https://).
- Keep your phone and apps updated to avoid security vulnerabilities.
- Be cautious of unknown links in emails, SMS, or WhatsApp messages.
- Log out from shared/public computers after use.
- Review app permissions and avoid granting unnecessary access.

Financial / UPI Safety
- Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or bank details with anyone—even if they claim to be from the bank.
- Remember: Receiving money does NOT require scanning a QR code or entering a PIN.
- Double-check payment requests before approving them.
- Use official apps only (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) and avoid third-party links.

Don’ts
- Don’t click on suspicious ads, pop-ups, or “too good to be true” offers.
- Don’t save passwords or banking info on public devices.
- Don’t accept unknown friend requests or messages blindly.

Report Cybercrime
- National Cyber Crime Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in  
- Helpline Number: 1930
