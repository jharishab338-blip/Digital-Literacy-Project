 Do’s
. Think before you post—ensure your content is respectful and appropriate
. Protect your privacy—adjust account settings and limit personal information
. Verify information before sharing to avoid spreading fake news
. Use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication
. Be respectful and polite in comments and discussions

 Don’ts
. Don’t share sensitive personal details (address, phone number, passwords)
. Don’t engage in cyberbullying, trolling, or online arguments
. Don’t believe or forward unverified news or rumors
. Don’t overshare your daily activities or location publicly
. Don’t click on suspicious links or unknown attachments
